new--Create & Run
In phpMyAdmin, click New on the left menu.

Type the name: lx_accounting and click the Create button.

Click on the SQL tab at the top of the screen.

Copy the entire text from your setup.sql file in VS Code, paste it into the big box in phpMyAdmin, and click Go at the bottom right.

Access the application in your browser: http://localhost/accounting/public/.

Click Parties > Sync Sellers to pull mock platform sellers into the accounting records.
Create your first Tax Invoice and check that intra-state vs inter-state tax amounts calculate accurately on the dashboard.
